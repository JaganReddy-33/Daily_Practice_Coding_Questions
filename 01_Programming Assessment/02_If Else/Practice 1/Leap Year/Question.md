# Leap Year

## Description

Determine whether a given year is a leap year or not.

A year is a leap year if:

The year is multiple of 4 and not a multiple of 100

The year is a multiple of 400

## Input Format

A single integer year (1000 <= year <= 3000) representing the year to be checked.

## Output Format

Print 'Leap year' if the year is a leap year, otherwise print 'Not a leap year'.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
2020
```

**Output**
```text
Leap year
```

### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
1900
```

**Output**
```text
Not a leap year
```
