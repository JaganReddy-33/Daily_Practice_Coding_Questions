# Pairs with Sum Greater than k

## Description

<p>Print all pairs of numbers from the input array whose sum is greater than the sum value k.</p>

## Input Format

<p>The first line contains an integer N, representing the size of the array.</p><p>The second line contains N space-separated integers, representing the elements of the array.</p><p>The third line contains a single integer k, representing the target sum value.</p>

## Output Format

<p>Print each pair of numbers on a new line, separated by a space.</p>

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
6
2 4 6 3 8 9
7
```

**Output**
```text
2 6
2 8
2 9
4 6
4 8
4 9
6 3
6 8
6 9
3 8
3 9
8 9
```
