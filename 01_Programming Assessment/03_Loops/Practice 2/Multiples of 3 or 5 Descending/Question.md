# Multiples of 3 or 5 Descending

## Description

Print numbers from N to 1 which are multiples of either 3 or 5 in descending order.

## Input Format

A single integer N (1 <= N <= 10^5) representing the starting natural number.

## Output Format

Print all numbers from N to 1 in descending order which are multiples of either 3 or 5, each separated by a space. If no common multiples print -1.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
20
```

**Output**
```text
20 18 15 12 10 9 6 5 3
```

### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
15
```

**Output**
```text
15 12 10 9 6 5 3
```
