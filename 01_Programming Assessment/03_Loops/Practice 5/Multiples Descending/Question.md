# Multiples Descending

## Description

Given three positive integers N1, N2, and M, print all multiples of M from N2 to N1 in descending order.

## Input Format

Three integers N1, N2, and M (1 <= N1 <= 10^5, 1 <= N2 <= 10^5, 1 <= M <= 10^5) representing the starting and ending natural numbers, and the base number.

## Output Format

Print all multiples of M between N2 and N1 in descending order, each separated by a space.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5 20 3
```

**Output**
```text
18 15 12 9 6
```

### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
10 30 5
```

**Output**
```text
30 25 20 15 10
```
