# Multiples Descending

## Description

<p>Given three positive integers N1, N2, and M, print all multiples of M from N2 to N1 in descending order.</p>

## Input Format

<p>Three integers N1, N2, and M (1 &lt;= N1 &lt;= 10^5, 1 &lt;= N2 &lt;= 10^5, 1 &lt;= M &lt;= 10^5) representing the starting and ending natural numbers, and the base number.</p>

## Output Format

<p>Print all multiples of M between N2 and N1 in descending order, each separated by a space.</p>

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5 20 3
```

**Output**
```text
18 15 12 9 6
```


### Sample Case 2

**Input**

**Output**
