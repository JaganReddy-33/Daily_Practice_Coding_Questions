# Multiples of M

## Description

Given three positive integers N1, N2, and M, print all multiples of M from N1 to N2.

## Input Format

Three integers N1, N2, and M (1 <= N1 <= 10^5, 1 <= N2 <= 10^5, 1 <= M <= 10^5) representing the starting and ending natural numbers, and the base number.

## Output Format

Print all multiples of M between N1 and N2, each separated by a space.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5 20 3
```

**Output**
```text
6 9 12 15 18
```

### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
10 30 5
```

**Output**
```text
10 15 20 25 30
```
