# Common Factors of n and m

## Description

<p>Print the common factors of two positive integers n and m.</p>

## Input Format

<p>Two positive integers n and m separated by a space.</p>

## Output Format

<p>Common factors separated by a space.</p>

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
10 25
```

**Output**
```text
1 5
```


### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
8 12
```

**Output**
```text
1 2 4
```
