# Prime Numbers between n1 and n2

## Description

<p>Print all prime numbers between n1 and n2.</p>

## Input Format

<p>Two positive integers n1 and n2 separated by a space.</p>

## Output Format

<p>Prime numbers separated by a space.</p>

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
12 40
```

**Output**
```text
13 17 19 23 29 31 37
```


### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
90 120
```

**Output**
```text
97 101 103 107 109 113
```
