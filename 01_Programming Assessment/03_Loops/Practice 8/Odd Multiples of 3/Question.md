# Odd Multiples of 3

## Description

Given two positive integers N1 and N2, print all the odd multiples of 3 from N1 to N2 in ascending order.

## Input Format

Two integers N1 and N2 (1 <= N1, N2 <= 10^5) representing the starting and ending natural numbers.

## Output Format

Print all the odd multiples of 3 between N1 and N2 in ascending order, each separated by a space.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5 20
```

**Output**
```text
9 15
```

### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
10 30
```

**Output**
```text
15 21 27
```
