# Multiples of 3 and 5

**Question ID:** `661ad0b95028dc4e9d17fffd`

## Description

Given two positive integers N1 and N2, print all multiples of 3 and 5 from N2 to N1 in descending order.

## Input Format

- Two integers N1 and N2 (1 <= N1, N2 <= 10^5) representing the starting and ending natural numbers.

## Output Format

- Print all the multiples of 3 and 5 between N2 and N1 in descending order, each separated by a space.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5 20
```

**Output**
```text
20 18 15 12 10 9 6 5
```

### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
10 30
```

**Output**
