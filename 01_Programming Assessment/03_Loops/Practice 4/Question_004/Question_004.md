# Multiples of 3, 5, and 7

**Question ID:** `661acf6bf51c6d596b0878a9`

## Description

Given two positive integers N1 and N2, print all multiples of 3, 5, and 7 from N1 to N2 in ascending order.

## Input Format

Two integers N1 and N2 (1 <= N1 <= 10^5, 1 <= N2 <= 10^5) representing the starting and ending natural numbers.

## Output Format

Print all multiples of 3, 5, and 7 between N1 and N2 in ascending order, each separated by a space.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5 30
```

**Output**
```text
5 6 7 9 10 12 14 15 18 20 21 24 25 27 28 30
```

10 40
