# Even Elements Sum

## Description

<p>Write a program to find the sum of all even elements present in an array.</p>

## Input Format

<p>First line contains a single integer N. Next line contains N space separated integer values.</p>

## Output Format

<p>Print sum of all even elements in an array.</p>

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5
1 4 6 3 10
```

**Output**
```text
20
```


### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
6
1 2 3 4 5 6
```

**Output**
```text
12
```


### Sample Case 3

**Input**
```text
5
2 4 6 8 10
```

**Output**
```text
30
```


### Sample Case 4

**Input**
```text
4
1 3 5 7
```

**Output**
```text
0
```


### Sample Case 5

**Input**
```text
3
-2 -4 -6
```

**Output**
```text
-12
```


### Sample Case 6

**Input**
```text
5
0 2 4 6 8
```

**Output**
```text
20
```


### Sample Case 7

**Input**
```text
4
10 20 30 40
```

**Output**
```text
100
```
