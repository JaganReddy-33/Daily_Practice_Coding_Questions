# Multiples of 3, 5, and 7 Descending

**Question ID:** `661acf7c5028dc4e9d179c83`

## Description

Given two positive integers N1 and N2, print all multiples of 3, 5, and 7 from N2 to N1 in descending order.

## Input Format

Two integers N1 and N2 (1 <= N1 <= 10^5, 1 <= N2 <= 10^5) representing the starting and ending natural numbers.

## Output Format

Print all multiples of 3, 5, and 7 between N2 and N1 in descending order, each separated by a space.

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
5 30
```

**Output**
```text
30 28 27 25 24 21 20 18 15 14 12 10 9 7 6 5
```

### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text
10 40
```

**Output**
```text
40 39 36 35 33 30 28 27 25 24 21 20 18 15 14 12 10
```
