# Multiples of Two & Three

## Description

<p>Write a program to print all the numbers which are multiples of either 2 or 3 till N.</p>

## Input Format

<p>First line consists of a positive integer n</p>

## Output Format

<p>Print the number from 2 to n space separated multiples of 2 and 3 integers.</p>

## Sample Cases

### Sample Case 1

**Input**
```text
15
```

**Output**
```text
2 3 4 6 8 9 10 12 14 15
```


### Sample Case 2

**Input**
```text

```

**Output**
```text

```
